## ✨ **Features**

### **1. Dual Signature Blocks**
- **Left Side:** Prepared By (Analyst)
  - Name line
  - Signature line
  - Date line

- **Right Side:** Approved By (QA Manager)
  - Name line
  - Signature line
  - Date line

### **2. Professional Formatting**
- ✅ Bordered signature boxes
- ✅ Clear labels and underlines
- ✅ Proper spacing between sections
- ✅ Automatic page break if needed

### **3. Regulatory Compliance Notice**
- Centered footer text
- References cGMP and 21 CFR Part 11
- Italic gray text for professional appearance

---

## 🎯 **Purpose**

### **Prepared By (Analyst):**
- The person who performed the tests
- Enters the test results
- Certifies the accuracy of the data

### **Approved By (QA Manager):**
- Quality Assurance Manager
- Reviews and approves the results
- Authorizes release of the COA
- **Required for regulatory compliance**

---

## 📐 **Technical Details**

### **Positioning:**
- Appears after the test results table
- 20px spacing from previous content
- Automatic page break if less than 60px space remaining

### **Dimensions:**
- Each signature box: 80px wide × 30px tall
- 10px gap between boxes
- Total width: ~170px (fits side-by-side)

### **Text Sizes:**
- Section title "Approvals": 12pt bold
- Box labels: 10pt bold
- Signature lines: 9pt normal
- Compliance notice: 8pt italic

---

## 🔒 **Regulatory Compliance**

### **Why This Matters:**

**cGMP (Current Good Manufacturing Practice):**
- Requires documented evidence of testing
- Mandates dual verification (analyst + QA)
- Ensures traceability and accountability

**21 CFR Part 11:**
- FDA regulation for electronic records
- Requires signatures for approval
- Ensures data integrity

### **What the Signature Section Provides:**
1. ✅ **Traceability** - Who prepared and who approved
2. ✅ **Accountability** - Named individuals responsible
3. ✅ **Date Tracking** - When prepared and approved
4. ✅ **Audit Trail** - Physical evidence of review
5. ✅ **Compliance** - Meets regulatory requirements

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **For Printed COAs:**
1. Generate and print the COA
2. Analyst signs and dates the left box
3. QA Manager reviews results
4. QA Manager signs and dates the right box
5. File the signed COA with batch records

### **For Electronic COAs:**
- The signature boxes serve as placeholders
- Can be filled electronically with e-signature systems
- Or printed and signed manually
